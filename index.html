Terms and Conditions

1. Information We Collect

  • Your name, email, and phone number (for account and booking purposes).
  • Payment details (processed securely by trusted providers; not stored on our servers).
  • Travel preferences and booking history.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process bookings and send tickets/confirmations.
  • To contact you with updates or provide customer support.
  • To improve our services and personalize your experience.

3. Sharing of Data

  • Booking details are shared only with our travel partners (bus operators, airlines, hotels, etc.) so they can provide your service.
  • We never sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Security

  • All sensitive data is encrypted and kept secure.
  • Payments are processed securely by Paystack, Flutterwave, or other trusted gateways.

5. Your Rights

  • You can request to update or delete your account/data at any time.
  • You can unsubscribe from marketing emails or SMS at any time.

6. Consent

By creating an account, you agree to these terms and conditions.
